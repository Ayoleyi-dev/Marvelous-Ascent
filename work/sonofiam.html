Delivered website · Digital presence

Giving a creative identity a home online.

An artist’s work deserves more than a page of scattered links. SonofIAM needed a digital experience where visitors could discover the work, explore its disciplines and understand how to connect or buy.

Visit public website ↗Discuss a website project
01 / THE NEED

Make the art easy to explore.

A distinctive visual identity needed an organised online setting for presenting work, separating creative disciplines and giving visitors direct commercial and contact pathways.

02 / OUR CONTRIBUTION

Plan, structure and build.

The work included digital experience strategy, information architecture, front-end implementation, gallery and shop organisation and iterative delivery. The visible website is the primary proof.

03 / THE SYSTEM

More than a collection of pages.

  • A branded exhibition-style entry experience
  • Work taxonomy and gallery navigation
  • Shop and commercial pathways
  • Clear routes for enquiries and connection
04 / WHAT WE CAN VERIFY

The experience exists and can be explored.

Visitors can inspect the public website and its information architecture. We do not claim improvements in conversion, sales, SEO or long-term maintenance without appropriate measurement.

What comes after launch?

A thoughtful next measurement layer.

The next level is dependable website and commerce measurement: event definitions, traffic-source tagging, order matching where applicable, regular reporting and a clear review routine.

This is a proposed next phase, not a claim that measurement has been deployed and validated.
