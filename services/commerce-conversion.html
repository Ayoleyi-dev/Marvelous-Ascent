The problem

Does this sound familiar?

These are common symptoms, not claims about your business.

  • Customers encounter confusing checkout steps
  • Orders and stock updates need repetitive reconciliation
  • Traffic cannot be tied to completed enquiries or sales

What is included

Clear deliverables, not buzzwords.

Typical scope

  • Conversion journey review and basic event plan
  • Scoped checkout, forms or product-page improvements
  • Order-tracking and operational handoff design
  • A measurement and validation checklist

Scope boundaries

Technology choices, integration access, client responsibilities and required security controls are confirmed during discovery.

Tools considered: Analytics, spreadsheets and selected ecommerce or payment integrations

Timeline: Proposed after reviewing complexity, dependencies and access.

Pricing: Written project quote based on agreed scope.

Working method

From input to handover.

We define what success means before building and check outputs against agreed acceptance criteria.

  1. 01
    Audit

    Map the current journey, integrations and failure points.

  2. 02
    Prioritise

    Agree realistic changes and the metrics that could validate them.

  3. 03
    Pilot

    Implement a small release and verify it before expanding.

Practical questions

Before we begin.

Can you guarantee a conversion lift?

No. We can recommend and test improvements, but results depend on traffic, audience and implementation.

Do you handle customer payment details?

Any payment work should use appropriate hosted provider flows. We do not request raw card details.

What is a sensible first milestone?

A checkout or enquiry-journey audit with agreed high-priority fixes.
