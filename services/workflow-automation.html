Usually not. We evaluate your current tools first and suggest integration before replacement.

What happens when an automation fails?

The specification includes alerting, a human fallback and ownership where the integration supports it.

Will you automate sensitive decisions?

High-impact workflows need explicit approvals, access controls and human review before deployment.
