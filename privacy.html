Privacy and transparency

Your information deserves thoughtful handling.

We believe you should know what information a website asks for, what happens to it and who to contact. This notice describes the current website and a proposed enquiry system, and needs legal and operational review before launch.

Draft — not a compliance certification. This notice must be checked against the services actually connected at launch, relevant Nigerian data protection requirements and any jurisdictions served.

Who to contact

Marvelous Ascent is a founder-led business offering data, automation and web services. For privacy requests, corrections or deletion enquiries, email meet.ayoleyi@gmail.com.

Information you choose to share

Project enquiries may include your name, email, organisation, location, service interest, current tools, timeline, optional budget and a description of the work. Please do not include passwords, payment-card details, medical records or other sensitive customer information in an initial enquiry.

How the current project brief works

Until the secure submission service is configured, the guided project brief prepares an email in your device's email application. Nothing is sent automatically. You must review the draft and press Send in your email app. Messages sent this way pass through your email provider and ours and are stored according to those services' arrangements.

If we activate secure website submissions

We plan to use a server-side enquiry endpoint with anti-spam verification, minimum necessary contact details, restricted database access and a defined retention period. A new submission will require your explicit agreement to be contacted about the enquiry. When active, submission status will be shown on the form. The proposed database cleanup window is 90 days for lead records, subject to operational verification; email copies or subsequent client correspondence may have different retention rules. This section describes a planned system, not one that is already live.

Purposes for using information

  • Understanding your project and responding to your enquiry.
  • Agreeing and delivering services you choose to commission.
  • Maintaining appropriate operational and legal records.
  • Sending optional updates only where a separate marketing choice or other valid basis exists.

Newsletter and third-party services

The homepage includes an optional newsletter form with an existing third-party automation connection whose complete handling and unsubscribe workflow still require verification before a launch claim. Do not subscribe if you are uncomfortable sharing your email through this integration. We also use externally hosted fonts; their providers may receive technical request information. If anti-spam or hosting services are enabled later, this notice will be updated with the actual providers and purposes.

Cookies, site analytics and preferences

The site currently stores a local display-theme preference in your browser. No consent-based analytics deployment has been verified as part of this development phase. We will describe and request relevant consent for optional analytics or marketing technologies before enabling them where required.

Rights and requests

You can contact us to ask about personal information we hold, request correction or deletion where appropriate, or raise a concern. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate legal or operational reasons. We will review requests under applicable law and our verified processing arrangements.

Last draft update: 8 October 2026. This draft must be approved before being relied on as a final legal notice.
