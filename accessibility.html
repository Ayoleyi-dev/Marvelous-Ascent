Everyone should feel welcome

A website that is easier to use.

We're working to make Marvelous Ascent understandable and usable across devices, preferences and assistive technologies.

What we're working on

The site uses labelled navigation, semantic page structure, visible focus styles, keyboard-friendly menus, in-page shortcuts, reduced-motion considerations and responsive layouts. We test representative desktop, tablet and phone screens in both light and dark themes.

Known limitations

Not every page and third-party integration has completed a full independent accessibility audit. The marketing demos include illustrative visualisations; descriptions and interactive controls are designed to provide context, but some assistive technologies may present these differently. We have not claimed certification against a formal WCAG conformance level.

Tell us where something is difficult

If a link, form, contrast combination, keyboard interaction or piece of content is hard to use, please contact meet.ayoleyi@gmail.com. Include the page address, your device or browser if comfortable, and a short description of the barrier. Please avoid including medical or other sensitive personal details.

Alternative ways to reach us

You can email us directly rather than using the guided enquiry form. We can also discuss a simpler message format or alternative communication method where feasible.
