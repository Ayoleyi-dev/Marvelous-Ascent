Clear expectations

Good projects need clear agreements.

These draft terms explain the intent of the public website and how we approach proposals. They do not replace a project-specific agreement.

Draft for professional review. These terms are not a substitute for legal advice, an executed contract or verified consumer-protection and refund requirements.

Using this site

Marvelous Ascent shares general information about its services, selected work and illustrative demonstrations. You may browse these public pages for informational purposes. Please don't attempt unauthorised access, upload harmful content or misuse the enquiry and newsletter features.

Illustrations and results

Interactive charts, fictional workflow examples and sample dashboards are educational demonstrations. They are not real-time business data, evidence of a particular client's results or performance guarantees.

Enquiries aren't project orders

Sending a project enquiry does not automatically create a contractual relationship, guarantee availability or reserve a delivery date. We may request more details to determine fit before offering a proposal.

Scope, timeline and payment

Each agreed engagement should have a written statement of work or proposal identifying the deliverables, acceptance criteria, timelines, responsibilities, fees, milestones, payment terms and any ongoing support. Work does not begin simply because a prospective client completes the website form.

Revisions, cancellations and refunds

Revision rounds, cancellation arrangements, refunds, deposits and treatment of expenses must be covered in each applicable written agreement before payment is taken. We do not make universal refund or delivery promises on this website.

Client access and third-party tools

Clients remain responsible for providing authorised access and confirming rights to materials they supply. Where a third-party hosting, analytics, payment or automation provider is required, responsibilities and separate provider charges should be explained in the proposal.

Ownership and portfolio permission

Project deliverable ownership, licences, reuse of tools or templates, source files and portfolio attribution should be agreed per engagement. We do not assume permission to display private client files, identities or outcomes.

Security and limitations

No online system can promise zero risk or uninterrupted availability. Any relevant confidentiality, security, data-processing or support requirements should be written into the relevant project agreement. Website information may change as services develop.

Questions
